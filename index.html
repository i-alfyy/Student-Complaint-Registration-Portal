CLARIFICATIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding eligibility, documentation, and procedures.

Who can use this portal? ▼
Authorized EEC students, faculty, and staff with valid @eec.srmrmp.edu.in email addresses.
Can I register without college email? ▼
No. Access is restricted to @eec.srmrmp.edu.in email addresses only.
What types can I report? ▼
18 categories including academics, infrastructure, facilities, harassment, and more.
How do I track complaint? ▼
Use your complaint reference ID (like #EEC-2026-00125) in the tracking dashboard.
Can I attach evidence? ▼
Yes. Submit photos, PDFs, and documentation through encrypted cloud storage.
Who can see my complaint? ▼
Only authorized grievance officers and department heads based on role-based access.
What happens after submit? ▼
Your complaint is logged, assigned a reference ID, reviewed, and you receive updates.
Can I reopen complaint? ▼
Closed complaints can be reopened with new information. Contact the Helpdesk.
